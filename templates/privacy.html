Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026  ·  Compliant with GDPR & India DPDP Act 2023

✅ We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, why, and your rights over it.

1. Who We Are

MediBot is an AI-powered medical information service ("we", "us", or "our"). We take your privacy seriously and are committed to complying with applicable data protection laws, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for EU users.

2. What Data We Collect

Data Type What Exactly Why We Collect It Retention
Chat Messages Text of your questions and our AI responses Improving AI accuracy and safety 90 days
IP Address Your IP (for rate limiting) Preventing abuse and spam 24 hours
Images Skin/medical images you upload AI analysis — passed to vision API Not stored
Response Time How long the bot took to respond Performance monitoring 90 days

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • Your name, email address, or contact details (no account required)
  • Your location (beyond country-level via IP)
  • Financial or payment information
  • Any data from third-party social media platforms
  • Cookies for tracking or profiling purposes

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the data collected to:

  • Generate accurate AI responses to your health questions
  • Improve the quality and safety of the AI system
  • Detect and prevent misuse, abuse, or emergency situations
  • Monitor system performance and uptime

We do NOT sell your data to third parties. We do NOT use your data for advertising.

5. Third-Party Services

MediBot uses the following third-party services, which may receive some of your data:

  • OpenRouter.ai — processes your text queries through AI language models. Their Privacy Policy applies.
  • Pinecone — stores the medical knowledge base (no personal user data). Their Privacy Policy applies.
  • NVIDIA / Vision API — processes uploaded images for analysis. Do not upload personally identifiable images.

6. Data Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Rate limiting to prevent unauthorized bulk access
  • Security headers (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options) on all responses
  • No plaintext storage of sensitive information
  • Regular security reviews

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR and India's DPDP Act, you have the following rights:

  • Right to Access — Request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Right to Deletion — Request deletion of your interaction logs
  • Right to Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Right to Object — Object to processing of your data
  • Right to Portability — Request your data in a machine-readable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

MediBot is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

9. Image Data Handling

Images you upload are:

  • Sent to the AI vision API in real-time for analysis
  • Not permanently stored on our servers
  • Processed only to generate the health information response

Please do NOT upload images containing your face, medical ID, or any personally identifiable information.

10. Cookies

MediBot uses only a single session storage item (medibot_disclaimer_v1) to remember that you have accepted the disclaimer in your current browser session. This is stored locally in your browser and is cleared when you close the tab. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top. Continued use of MediBot after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any privacy-related questions, concerns, or requests, please contact us through the chatbot interface or at the contact information listed on our platform. We will respond within 30 business days.
